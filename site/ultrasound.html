Data and measurements

Two U-Net models marked muscle boundaries (aponeuroses) and fibre bundles (fascicles). Line fits turned these masks into measurements; ruler ticks converted pixels to millimetres.

The metric averages absolute errors in pennation angle, fascicle length and muscle thickness, divided by 6 degrees, 12 mm and 3 mm, respectively. Training used 1,048 boundary and 2,761 fascicle image-mask pairs; a separate 35-image expert benchmark checked measurements. The 309 test images included devices, muscles and subjects absent from training.

The first local scorer supplied correct ruler scale and adjusted average fascicle length to match the labels, hiding scale and calibration errors. After removing that adjustment, locally preferred geometry changes also worsened public scores.

Scale, geometry and calibration

The models were trained on 9 June 2026 and their weights were reused. Later files inherited earlier ruler corrections, angle and length calibration, and boundary selection. The July threshold tests branched from the 14 June submission.
